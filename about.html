Who We Are

Building a Reliable Global
Medicine Supply Chain

Rajkamal Wing Pharma Pvt. Ltd. is a fast-growing pharmaceutical export company based in India, dedicated to bridging the gap between high-quality manufacturers and global healthcare markets.

We specialize in sourcing and exporting a comprehensive range of pharmaceutical formulations, including injectables, tablets, capsules, and specialty medicines. With several years of experience in the pharmaceutical industry, we serve export markets including the Philippines and other international destinations through a reliable global supply chain.

Our team is driven by a commitment to quality, integrity, and long-term partnerships. By working with certified manufacturing facilities, we ensure that all products meet international standards and regulatory requirements.

  • Global Pharmaceutical Exporter
  • WHO-GMP Certified Supplier
  • Reliable Pharma Partner
  • Core specialization in injectables, tablets, capsules, and specialty medicines
